Mission Board Architecture

Reachable at mission-board.com.mx from anywhere — with zero inbound ports opened at home. Cloudflare reaches in through a tunnel the machine dials out.

  • host hyperion (Debian)
  • app Next.js + FastAPI
  • data MariaDB
  • auth Google OIDC

The pieces

1

Public entry — Cloudflare

mission-board.com.mx is managed in Cloudflare, which answers DNS and terminates TLS with a real public certificate, then hands the request to the tunnel. Because TLS ends at the edge, the origin can speak plain HTTP over loopback — no certificates to manage on hyperion.

2

The tunnel — cloudflared

cloudflared runs as a systemd service on hyperion (tunnel 16f8ff0d…). Its ingress maps the hostname to the local app; anything else falls through to a 404. The connection is outbound hyperion→Cloudflare, so nothing is opened on the router or LAN.

3

The app — Docker on loopback

The container project-management-mvp (Next.js served by FastAPI) is published as 127.0.0.1:8000 — loopback only, not on the LAN or the Tailscale tailnet. It joins the homelab-net Docker network to reach the database by name. The tunnel is the single door.

4

The database — shared MariaDB

Data lives in kanbanpmdb inside the shared homelab-db MariaDB container, reached with PyMySQL over homelab-net by container name. Data persists in the volume, so replacing the app container never touches it.

5

Authentication — Google OIDC

Sign-in is Google OAuth with an email allowlist: only added emails may enter. Since the app is publicly reachable, the allowlist is what keeps it private in practice.

The tunnel config

The whole ingress is four lines — ~/.cloudflared/config.yml on hyperion:

tunnel: 16f8ff0d-374d-44cc-9e4f-fefdda31adba
credentials-file: /etc/cloudflared/16f8ff0d-….json
ingress:
  - hostname: mission-board.com.mx
    service: http://localhost:8000
  - service: http_status:404

Why this shape

No inbound ports

The home network exposes nothing. The only channel is the outbound tunnel — no public IP or port-forward to attack.

TLS for free

Certificates are provisioned and renewed at Cloudflare's edge. Nothing to install or rotate on hyperion.

Loopback origin

Even inside the house the app isn't on the LAN or tailnet. The tunnel is the single entry point.

Auth gate

Public reachability is fine because Google plus the allowlist gate every request.

Two apps, two exposure models

hopeland2 — the other personal app on hyperion — is served privately over the Tailscale tailnet (Tailscale Serve), reachable only from allowed devices, with the work laptop denied by an ACL. Same box, opposite front door.
